## Why these scenarios apply

For each selected asset, show the object/field mapping, rule version, persona, integration dependency and positive/negative/boundary variants. Explain exclusions: invisible metadata, missing downstream access, unknown business policy or out-of-scope product. Count selected, reviewed, executed, passed, failed, blocked and not checked separately, with denominators.

## What the evidence establishes

Record target environment, actual identity, tested data scope, run and step outcomes, execution time, source/rule version and reviewed media. A successful API connection proves access; an existing field proves selected schema visibility; a reviewed business sentence proves an owner decision was recorded. Neither proves the workflow passed.

## What happens next

List each gap with owner, action and review date. Record approved exceptions, rollback ownership and deployment approval separately. Link the reviewed customer guide and the changes that require its refresh. End with one concrete next action for the customer rather than a generic confidence score.
