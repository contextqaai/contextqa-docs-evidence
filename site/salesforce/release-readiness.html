Three questions before a release

Does it apply?

Check official advisories against the intended org. Record sources and unknowns.

What can it affect?

Trace known dependencies to business rules, personas and connected systems.

What proves readiness?

Review current assertions in the correct environment, including failures and blocked checks.

A concrete integration deadline: Salesforce lists February 20, 2027 for retirement of the OAuth username-password flow. Applicability depends on the integrations actually using it. Official notice, checked November 14, 2026.

Build a reviewable evidence pack

Include the advisory or proposed change, affected rules, environment bindings, selected test results, unresolved gaps, owners and next actions. An old successful run or a different sandbox is not current readiness evidence.
