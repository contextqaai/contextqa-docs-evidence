## ContextQA-only documentation policy

Use ContextQA exclusively for browser interaction, including case setup, discovery, troubleshooting and acceptance. Configure saved journeys through ContextQA tools or its supported API. Do not open a Codex-controlled browser for setup or inspection. The user requires ContextQA to perform every product journey and produce its execution evidence. Do not use a separate browser driver, manual screenshots, copied documentation screenshots or generated UI images as workflow proof. Saved ContextQA cases/plans are the durable journeys. Use their run/case/step results and downloaded media; preserve provenance and deployment binding. Failed or stale runs must not replace the latest verified documentation. Keep immutable versions, text/media comparisons and the current approved copy. Historical manual-browser examples are excluded from acceptance. Synthetic offline transport fixtures are permitted only as explicitly labeled tests, never live proof.
